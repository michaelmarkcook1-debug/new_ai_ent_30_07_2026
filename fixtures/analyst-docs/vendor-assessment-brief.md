# Shell AI vendor assessment brief (sample document)

Status: preloaded sample for the demo. Content is illustrative.

## Purpose

Frame the platform decision for enterprise AI adoption across trading,
maintenance, safety assurance and back-office functions.

## Shortlist position

Three configurations are under assessment:

1. Frontier lab API plus incumbent cloud (best model quality, two contracts,
   orchestration owned in house).
2. Single hyperscaler stack (one throat to choke, deepest existing
   agreements, model ceiling accepted).
3. Dual frontier strategy behind a model-agnostic orchestration layer
   (maximum leverage, highest integration cost).

## Working conclusions

- The orchestration layer must stay model-agnostic whichever configuration
  is chosen; agent frameworks are the new lock-in surface.
- Safety-critical use cases route through the strictest applicable regime
  (EU AI Act high-risk) by default.
- Delivery partner selection should follow the AI readiness ranking rather
  than headline day rates.
- Baseline measurement is mandatory before scale-up funding: maintenance
  and trading have credible baselines, back office does not yet.

## Open questions

- Which configuration best fits the governance posture in Trust Rank?
- What contractual terms cover model deprecation and price movement?
- Where does the workforce plan need to lead the technology plan?
