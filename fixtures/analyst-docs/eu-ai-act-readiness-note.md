# EU AI Act readiness note (sample document)

Status: preloaded sample for the demo. Content is illustrative.

## Position

The EU AI Act is in force with phased application: general-purpose AI
obligations from August 2025, high-risk system requirements phasing through
2026 and 2027. Safety-critical industrial deployments are likely to qualify
as high-risk and carry conformity, documentation and human-oversight duties.

## Readiness gaps identified

1. The AI use-case registry covers roughly two thirds of known deployments;
   completion is the pacing item before the next audit cycle.
2. Deployment gates exist for customer-facing AI but not yet for industrial
   control adjacency.
3. Supplier contracts predate the Act's deployer duties; AI clauses need
   standardising across integrator and vendor agreements.

## Actions

- Complete the registry, then classify every use case against the Act's
  risk tiers.
- Gate high-risk classes behind documented conformity checks with named
  accountability per production model.
- Track national supervision differences (Germany adds works council
  co-determination; France adds active data protection guidance).
