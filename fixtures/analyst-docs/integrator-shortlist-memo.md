# Integrator shortlist memo (sample document)

Status: preloaded sample for the demo. Content is illustrative.

## Context

Early scale-up will be partner-delivered while the internal applied-AI
bench grows. The delivery channel decision matters as much as the platform
decision this year.

## Selection criteria agreed

1. AI readiness score in the live ranking, weighted first.
2. Evidenced agentic delivery capability, not proof-of-concept counts.
3. Platform certifications across the chosen vendor configuration.
4. Regulated-industry delivery references in energy or adjacent sectors.
5. Workforce transition support capability alongside build capability.

## Notes

- The catalogue's top quartile on AI readiness is competitive on price for
  multi-year commitments.
- Certification depth varies sharply by AI platform; check the integrator
  by platform matrix before shortlisting.
- Contract against outcomes with measurable baselines wherever a baseline
  exists; time-and-materials only for discovery work.
